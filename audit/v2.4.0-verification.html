Live specimenMẫu trực tiếp · flip version, theme and element

The fixture the checks measure. Compare v2.3.1 and v2.4.0 side by side: nested dark inside light, glass over aurora, rings in each Ngũ Hành scope, and Vietnamese display type.

01

Scoped nested theme & Liquid Glass re-computationbase/glass.css · tính lại token trong phạm vi lồng nhau

A data-theme="dark" block inside a light page (and the reverse) must resolve --_cs-border-subtle, --_cs-border-strong, --_cs-surface-scrim and the eight --cs-glass-*-{bg,border} materials exactly as a dark (or light) root would. The scrim must darken dark surfaces for modal separation.

02

Component polish & contract alignmentcomponents · controls · collections · shell

Loading and disabled buttons are distinguishable; dialogs use --cs-radius-lg as DESIGN.md specifies; invalid comboboxes paint the danger border; and selected/active markers carry a 2px --cs-accent-strong perimeter in light, dark and every element scope.

03

Vietnamese diacritic clearance & tooltip wrappingtypography · feedback · data — “Hệ thống Quản trị Nhân sự & Pháp lý Doanh nghiệp — Đổi mới sáng tạo”

Display type under :lang(vi) gets 1.22 line-height so stacked marks (Ệ, Ổ, Ị) clear the line above; clearance is measured from the rendered glyph ink. Badges and multi-line tooltips are checked with the same string.

Method & evidencePhương pháp & bằng chứng

Baseline. The v2.3.1 tag's flattened dist/styles.min.css and the tagged guideline, deck and template files are stored in audit/baseline-v2.3.1/. v2.4.0 uses the live source styles.css closure.

  • Colours are compared after the browser resolves them; scrims are composited in sRGB over the scope's page colour.
  • Non-text contrast uses WCAG relative luminance; a marker passes when its fill or its ring reaches 3:1 against the panel.
  • Diacritic clearance = computed line-height − (ink ascent + descent) of the VN string at display size.

Scope and limits. Seven dimensions are reconstructed from the v2.3.1 remediation scope (the original scorecard is not in the repository). Results reflect this browser, its fonts and zoom; they do not replace screen-reader, print-to-PDF or human visual acceptance.
