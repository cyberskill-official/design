Method & evidencePhương pháp & bằng chứng

Sources inspected. DESIGN.md, styles.css (+ dist/styles.css, the flattened closure), base/*.css, tokens/*.css, audit/v2.4.0-verification.html and audit/verify.js from cyberskill-official/design.

  • Colours are compared after the browser resolves them (rgb, color(srgb), oklab); translucent rings are composited in sRGB over their backdrop.
  • Non-text contrast uses WCAG relative luminance; progress passes when its fill or its 1px inset ring reaches 3:1 against the track.
  • Hover rules are read from the CSSOM and applied to a probe inside the dark default scope; pseudo-elements are measured with getComputedStyle(el, "::before").
  • Diacritic clearance = computed line-height − (ink ascent + descent) measured from the rendered glyphs via Canvas.

Scope and limits. Results reflect this browser, its fonts, pointer type and zoom; they do not replace screen-reader, print-to-PDF or human visual acceptance. The v2.4.0 and v2.3.1 baselines are flattened from the tagged styles.css closures (25 files each); DESIGN.md and the brand assets for the baselines are fetched from the same tags.

Files. audit/fixture-v2.5.0.html (probe DOM + measurer) · audit/verify-v2.5.0.js (checks, scoring, rendering) · audit/baseline-2.4.0/ · audit/baseline-2.3.1/ · audit/v2.4.0-verification.html + verify.js + fixture.html (the ported baseline suite).
