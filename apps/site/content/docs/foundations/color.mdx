## How much of each

| Surface | Neutrals | Ink | Gold | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product screens | ~88% | ~8% | under 3% | ~1% |
| Marketing pages | ~70% | ~20% | up to 8% | ~2% |

Gold works because it is rare: one key action, highlight or AI moment per view.

## Status colors

Every pair stays at least ΔE 15 apart for normal vision and ΔE 8 for red-blind and green-blind vision. Status is still never shown by color alone: chips, alerts and toasts carry an icon and text.

<StatusColors />

## Chart palette

Series take colors in this fixed order and keep them when filters change. Each color passes 3:1 on the chart surface and stays at least ΔE 15 from every status color, so a chart never reads as an alert.

## Rules

- Never put white text on gold, and don't use bright gold as text on light backgrounds: use `--accent-text`.
- No gradients or glows on brand colors.
- Don't hard-code hex values in components; the CSS check blocks it.
