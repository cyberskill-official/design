| Role | Family | Weights | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | **Manrope** | 600, 700, 800 | Compact and confident at large sizes |
| Body and UI | **Be Vietnam Pro** | 400, 500, 600, 700, italic | Designed with Vietnamese diacritics in mind |
| Data and code | **JetBrains Mono** | 400, 500, 600 | Clear digits for IDs, amounts and code |

All three are free under the SIL Open Font License and served by Google Fonts.

## Scale

Apply with the `cs-*` classes or the `--text-*` tokens.

## Rules

- Minimum 12px for running text, 11px for monospace labels.
- Keep paragraphs to about 60–75 characters per line.
- Use `text-wrap: balance` on headings (the `cs-h*` classes do this).
