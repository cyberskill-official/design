A11y CSSOM gate

Bounded checks on the global accessibility CSS: declared coarse-pointer floors, static focus-ring rules, reduced-motion and high-contrast selectors. These source checks do not prove actual hit areas or rendered focus behavior. The pinned local axe engine checks accessible naming of the 20 mounted fixture controls; empty labels, broken references, missing fixtures or an unavailable engine fail. This is not a complete WCAG or screen-reader assessment. Use the interaction and touch regressions plus human review for their separate scopes. Verdict on window.__a11ycss.
