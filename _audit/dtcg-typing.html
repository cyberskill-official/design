DTCG typing gate

CyberSkill emitted-value profile pinned to DTCG 2025.10 Format and Color Community Group reports. Checks all token values, typed aliases (including shadow fields), cycles and provenance counts. Supports the seven emitted types and sRGB; unsupported types, $ref and $extends fail. This is not general reader conformance or proof of a design-tool import. Scope overrides and raw CSS extensions require separate adapters. Verdict on window.__dtcgtyping.
